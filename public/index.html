Slopcore Globe

A music player that plays Ai/Si music videos from X. A tap to see the newest, most liked, most shared, leaderboard and your own playlists.

Coming soon to iOS and Android
  • A globe full of music videos

    A selection of Ai/Si-generated music videos that creators post publicly on X, collected in one Library and shown on a spinning globe. Users can suggest posts to be added to library.

  • Sort it your way

    Newest, most liked, most shared or Leaderboard. Tap a post and it plays, with full credit to its creator and a link to the post on X.

  • A visualizer that moves with the music

    Bars, radial or rings around the globe, in time with the post that is playing.

  • Your own playlists

    Free to watch, no login needed. An optional Personal Playlists subscription lets you build, order and name up to 10 of your own playlists.
