Slopcore Globe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

The short version: Slopcore Globe needs no login, has no ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell your data. The app uses a random install ID, sent only when you save, suggest, report, tag, make a playlist, send feedback or delete your data, never with the routine refresh of the globe. Posts and videos come from X, so X can see your device when they load. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. The details are below.

Who we are

Slopcore Globe is an app for iPhone and Android made by Magnus Kyltveit, an independent developer in Norway. The Android app on Google Play is published by Kyltveit ENK (org. no. 938613125), Magnus Kyltveit's sole proprietorship. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Magnus Kyltveit.

  • Website: https://slopcoreglobe.com/

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven), Magnus Kyltveit is the data controller for the personal data described here. The best way to reach us about privacy is by email at hello@slopcoreglobe.com.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and you don't need an account to browse, watch or listen.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos or camera.
  • It contains no advertising, no advertising ID, no third-party analytics and no crash-reporting SDK of its own.
  • It does not sign in to X, and never posts, likes, follows or sends messages on X for you.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you or to train AI models.

Your random install ID

The first time you open the app it creates a random ID (a UUID) and stores it on your phone. It is not linked to your name, phone number, Apple ID, Google account or advertising ID. The app does not send it when it refreshes the globe (about once a minute) or when you just look at posts. It is sent to our server only when you do one of these: save a post, make or change a playlist, add a private tag, suggest a post, report a post, send feedback, or use Delete my data. That lets the server tell which saves, playlists, tags, suggestions and reports come from the same install. Deleting the app deletes the ID from your phone; a reinstall gets a new one.

Like any internet service, our server provider sees your device's IP address when the app connects and may keep short-lived technical logs for operation and security. We don't use IP addresses to identify you.

Data that stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device and are never sent to us: the pictures you choose for your playlists (picked from your photo library, kept in the app's private storage, never uploaded; the app only asks for access to the photos you pick), the order of your playlists, creators you blocked and posts you hid in Slopcore Globe, your settings (layout, card effects, visualizer, volume, clock), the post you were last playing, and a cached copy of your subscription status. Deleting the app deletes them.

  • Blocking a creator only hides their posts in Slopcore Globe on your phone. It does not block them on X and nothing is sent to us or to X.
  • Motion sensor: if Card effects are on, the app reads the phone's tilt to animate the cards. The readings are used on the phone in real time and never stored or sent. You can turn this off in Settings › Card effects.
  • Now Playing and media buttons: in the landscape player the app shows the post's text, @handle and picture in your phone's Now Playing controls (lock screen, car, headphones). This stays on your device and the devices you connect it to.
  • Screen mirroring: the app checks whether your screen is mirrored so the optional "keep video in app while mirroring" setting can work. Nothing is stored or sent.

The sound visualizer

On both iPhone and Android the visualizer follows the sound of the app's own audio (the video that is playing), analysed on the phone in real time. The app does not use the microphone on either platform and never asks for microphone permission. The levels are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Posts and videos from X

Everything you watch in Slopcore Globe is a public post on X. Our server stores public information about those posts (post ID, text, creator name, @handle, profile picture link, date, public like/repost/view counts and media links), which we get from the X API.

  • To show and play posts, the app loads pictures and videos directly from X's servers (for example twimg.com). The app draws each post itself (it does not embed X's web page). When this happens, X receives your IP address and standard device information, and X's handling of that data is covered by X's Privacy Policy.
  • Links like "View on X" or a creator's @handle open X's app or website, outside Slopcore Globe.

Personal Playlists subscription

The optional subscription is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see your card or billing details. The app asks the store on your phone whether your subscription is active, and keeps that answer on the phone for up to 7 days so it keeps working offline. We do not send your purchase details to our server. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the purchase.

Sign in with X (owner only)

The app does not sign users in to X and does not ask you to log in to anything. The only exception is a hidden admin screen that only the owner of Slopcore Globe uses to review suggested posts, feedback and tags. It signs in with the owner's own X account (OAuth 2.0). Our server checks the X account ID, and only the owner's account unlocks it. If anyone else tries, nothing is unlocked and the X access token is not stored.

Optional Sign in with Apple (iPhone)

A future version of the iPhone app may offer an optional Sign in with Apple, only to sync your playlist across your devices. If you use it, Apple shares a stable user ID with us and, depending on your choice, your email address or an Apple private relay address. Supabase stores this account and links it to the install IDs of the phones you sign in on. We use it only to sync your playlist. You can delete the account at any time in the app (Settings › Delete account), which removes the account and its links; or email us. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple in your Apple ID settings.

Why we can use your data (legal bases)

  • Providing the app and features you ask for (your playlist, the subscription, an optional account): performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
  • Reviewing suggestions and reports, moderation, rate limits and security: our legitimate interest in running a safe service, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Motion sensor effects: you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time; the data never leaves your phone.

Service providers and where data is stored

  • Supabase (database and server) processes the server data above on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Our project is hosted in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Apple and Google deliver the app and handle purchases. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.
  • X provides the posts, pictures and videos, as described above. X is based in the United States and acts under its own privacy policy.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA (for example by Apple, Google or X), the provider relies on the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy of it (data portability). You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Deleting your data

  • Delete my data: tap Settings › Delete my data in the app. This deletes everything on our server that is tied to your install ID: your saved posts, playlists, private tags, post suggestions, reports and feedback. It also clears the same things, and the install ID, from your phone. It happens right away, and you don't need to contact us.
  • To remove a single post from a playlist, untick it under Saved in on the post.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone, including the install ID, your settings and your playlists and their pictures. (Do this after Delete my data if you also want your server data gone, because the app needs the install ID to find it.)
  • Rate-limit logs are deleted automatically after 2 days.
  • For any other request about your rights, such as access to or a copy of your data, email hello@slopcoreglobe.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Slopcore Globe is for people aged 13 and up. This matches the age of digital consent in Norway (13) and X's minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we find out that we have, we will delete it. If you think a child under 13 has sent us personal data (for example in a report note), email hello@slopcoreglobe.com and we will delete it.

Security

The app talks to our server only over encrypted HTTPS. The database uses row-level security: the app's public key can only read the public Library and its own rows, and reports and suggestions cannot be read back through the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.
