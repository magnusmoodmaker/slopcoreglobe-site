Slopcore Globe

A music player that plays AI/SI music videos from X. A tap to see the newest, most liked, and shared, a leaderboard and your own playlists.

Coming soon to iOS and Android
  • A globe full of music videos

    AI-generated music videos that creators post publicly on X, collected in one Library and shown on a spinning globe.

  • Sort it your way

    Newest, most liked, most shared or most seen. Tap a post and it plays, with full credit to its creator and a link to the post on X.

  • A visualizer that moves with the music

    Bars, radial or rings around the globe, in time with the post that is playing.

  • Your own playlists

    Free to watch, no login needed. An optional Personal Playlists subscription lets you build, order and name up to 10 of your own playlists.
